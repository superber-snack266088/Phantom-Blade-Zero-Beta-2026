## 🕹️ About

Phantom Blade Zero is an upcoming single-player action RPG from S-GAME, blending traditional wuxia, dark fantasy, folk horror, and kungfu-punk style. Play as Soul, a warrior with only 66 days to uncover a deadly consalternative across the Wulin and Phantom World. The game launches October 29, 2026, and this beta test gives selected players a chance to help shape the experience while receiving a free game key.

Phantom Blade Zero is made for players who enjoy challenging single-player action RPGs, martial-arts combat, dark fantasy worlds, and cinematic wuxia storytelling.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- ⚔️ **Cinematic Kung Fu** — Master fast, fluid combat built around dodges, counters, parries, and precise strikes.
- 🗡️ **Extensive Arsenal** — Experiment with more than 30 main weapons and powerful Phantom Edges.
- 🌑 **Dark Wuxia World** — Explore the mysterious Wulin and Phantom World through a dramatic martial-arts adventure.
- 🩸 **Soul’s Countdown** — Uncover the truth before Soul’s remaining 66 days run out.
- 🛠️ **Build Your Style** — Upgrade weapons, combine accessories, and shape a combat setup that fits your playstyle.
- 🎭 **Branching Discovery** — Complete side quests, uncover secrets, and deepen your understanding of the Wulin.
- 🎁 **Beta Tester Rewards** — Join the testing community and receive a free game key for participating.
